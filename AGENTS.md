# Stela Agent Instructions

`PoT.md` is final authority. This file supplies repository-local execution guidance and creates no parallel canon.

## Delegated-Agent Transparency

The Operator grants standing authorization inside Stela for bounded subagent use when the Integrator or Contractor determines that delegation materially improves speed, isolation, review, or continuity.

Before spawning or managing a delegated agent, read and follow `docs/MANUAL.md#delegated-agent-transparency`. Report every spawn, scope, authority boundary, material result, exact descendant census, and final disposition explicitly to the Operator. Provider interfaces, tool output, and logs do not substitute for plain-language disclosure.

The spawning role remains immediately accountable for its descendants. The Integrator retains operation-wide accountability for delegated activity. A spawned descendant does not qualify as an independent Auditor of its parent.
