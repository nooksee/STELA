## CbC Preflight Questions

**Q1: Is structural prevention viable instead of detection?**
No. A record is binary and no generator produces it; the digest list is the only statement of what the bytes are, so agreement between the list and the files can only be checked. The lint is a verify lane rather than a gates step so that it runs where the lane policy already routes packet-local checks (full verify, pull requests that touch the owned paths, certify for packets that change them). The dp self-test lane adds no new check; it routes an existing self-test (`bash tools/lint/dp.sh --test`) into full verify and into gates when its owned files change, so the next fixture break is visible before a packet trips on it.

**Q2: Does the enforcement complexity exceed 100 lines without justification?**
The lint is 199 lines including its six-fixture self-test and usage text; the check itself (parse, presence both ways, `sha256sum -c`) is under 80 lines. Two manifest lines, one registry row, one inventory expectation; no configuration beyond the lane fields the manifest already defines.

## Decision

Proceed with two verify lanes for DP-OPS-0270: dp-lint-self-test (standalone-full-only, DECISIONS.md 16) and records-digest (packet-local, the #412 sidecar question), the records lint with six self-test fixtures, its registry row and spec, and the inventory expectation.

## Consequence

A corrupted, missing, or unlisted research record fails full verify, pull-request gates that touch the records directory, and certify for packets that change it; a broken dp lint fixture fails full verify on every push to main and gates on pull requests that touch the dp lint or its templates. Proof at execution: the live lint passed on 156 records; appending one byte to one record produced one FAIL naming the record; the six self-test fixtures passed; a gates-mode probe with one records path selected the records lane and deferred the dp self-test lane for no path match. Allowlist enforcement requires this leaf to be present and allowlisted before certify runs when the CbC preflight is applicable.
