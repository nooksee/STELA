## Context

CbC Design Discipline Preflight record for packet DP-OPS-0275.
Tool or binary under review: tools/lint/response.sh

## CbC Preflight Questions

**Q1: Is structural prevention viable instead of detection?**
Partially, and taken where it applies. The schema itself is the structure: one header, seven sections in a fixed order, two closed value sets (`in_thread` or `fresh_thread`; `adopt`, `adapt`, `reject`, `pending`), one fixed authority sentence, and an adapter identifier that resolves against `ops/src/runbook/<adapter>.yaml`, so an unregistered adapter cannot land at all. What remains is detection of a malformed or incoherent artifact at ingress, which is the existing job of `tools/lint/response.sh`; a consult artifact is pasted by hand, and no binary renders it, so there is no earlier point at which to prevent the malformation.

**Q2: Does the enforcement complexity exceed 100 lines without justification?**
It exceeds 100 lines with justification. The two check functions are 141 lines; 455 lines were added in total with ten `--test` fixtures and the mode wiring, and 4 were removed. Seven section rules and one coherence rule each have a fixture that fails for its own reason, probed live before the self-test was trusted.

**Q3: Was structural prevention identified as viable but deferred?**
No. A rendered consult template under `ops/src/surfaces/` was considered and left out of scope until an adapter emits the envelope in practice; it would add a render path, not remove a detection.

**Q4: Complexity score (1–5) and rationale:**
Score: 2
One new mode in an existing mode-dispatch lint, built from the existing fenced-envelope extractor, the existing rejection helpers, and one section-body helper; no new dependency, no `rg`.

**Q5: Does a SoP note record any deferred structural prevention decision?**
Not required.

## Decision

Proceed with a consult mode in the response lint: one schema for every external runtime adapter, validated at the landing surface before any decision reads it.

## Consequence

`bash tools/lint/response.sh --test` passes with the ten consult fixtures and every earlier fixture; the Gemini research adapter, whose output is advisory by definition, has a landing surface and a validator. Allowlist enforcement requires this leaf to be present and allowlisted before certify runs when the CbC preflight is applicable.
