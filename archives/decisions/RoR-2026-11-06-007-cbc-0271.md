## CbC Preflight Questions

**Q1: Is structural prevention viable instead of detection?**
In part. The duplicate dual-scope check (one copy in `tools/lint/integrity.sh`, one in `ops/bin/certify`) could drift apart silently; replacing both with one helper in `ops/lib/scripts/common.sh` prevents that class of drift structurally, which is item 18. The check itself stays detection, as before. The dry-run mode adds no check; it stops certify after the validations it already performs, so an operator and a test can exercise the closeout preflight without a replay. The fixture runs that path on a synthetic packet in a copy of the working tree, so the code under test is the working tree and not a committed snapshot.

**Q2: Does the enforcement complexity exceed 100 lines without justification?**
No. common.sh gains 103 lines (two predicates moved in, one helper of 56 lines, comments); the integrity lint loses 53 and gains 14; certify loses 52 and gains 31 (the dry-run block is 16 of them); the test gains 152 lines for one fixture builder and two cases. Net enforcement logic is smaller than before; the test is the largest addition.

**Q3: Was structural prevention identified as viable but deferred?**
No. Nothing was deferred.

**Q4: Complexity score (1–5) and rationale:**
Score: 2
One shared helper with nameref arguments replacing two loops; one early-exit block in certify; one fixture that builds a real packet with the repository's own binaries.

**Q5: Does a SoP note record any deferred structural prevention decision?**
Not required. Nothing was deferred.

## Decision

Proceed with the shared dual-scope helper in common.sh used by the integrity lint and certify (DECISIONS.md 18), the certify --dry-run mode, and the certify dry-run fixture on a synthetic packet in tools/test/integrity.sh (DECISIONS.md 17).

## Consequence

The integrity lint and certify apply one dual-scope loop; a rule change lands once. `./ops/bin/certify --dry-run` reports closeout readiness without emitting a leaf. The packet-scope contract lane proves the certify preflight path on every full verify, every pull request that touches its owned files, and every certify. Proof at execution: 13 of 13 fixtures in 13 seconds, the dry run passing on a synthetic packet and failing closed at the integrity gate on an undeclared file; the live integrity lint and this packet's certify run on the shared helper. Allowlist enforcement requires this leaf to be present and allowlisted before certify runs when the CbC preflight is applicable.
