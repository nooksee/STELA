## Context

CbC Design Discipline Preflight record for packet DP-OPS-0268.
Tool or binary under review: tools/verify.sh (section 3, Filing Doctrine checks)

The packet lands the Operator's research corpus as 156 PDF records under `opt/_research/` with a digest list and a README. The first full verify run on the staged corpus reported 313 errors: 156 from the binary-file check and 157 from the non-markdown check for `opt/`. Section 3 of `tools/verify.sh` runs in all three verify modes, in the gates workflow, and inside certify, so the corpus could not certify or merge without an enforcement change. The Operator chose to admit `opt/_research/` as a records class inside this packet rather than a separate packet, a text form, or a catalog-only landing.

## CbC Preflight Questions

**Q1: Is structural prevention viable instead of detection?**
No. The check reads filesystem content, and no generator produces research records; detection is the only form, as the DP-OPS-0140 retrospective audit found for the whole tool (score C). The records class is written as a positive membership rule, three admitted name patterns under one directory, rather than a blanket exclusion, so an unexpected file under the records directory fails with its own message.

**Q2: Does the enforcement complexity exceed 100 lines without justification?**
No. One constant, one membership function, one loop over the records directory, and two `find` exclusions: 25 added lines, no configuration, no state.

**Q3: Was structural prevention identified as viable but deferred?**
No. Nothing was deferred.

**Q4: Complexity score (1–5) and rationale:**
Score: 1
One directory constant and one `case` pattern with three alternatives; the existing checks keep their behavior for every other `opt/` and `docs/` path.

**Q5: Does a SoP note record any deferred structural prevention decision?**
Not required. Nothing was deferred.

## Decision

Proceed with the records class in tools/verify.sh for DP-OPS-0268: `opt/_research/` admits `*.pdf`, `SHA256SUMS`, and `README.md`; the binary-file and non-markdown checks skip that directory; any other file inside it is a Filing Doctrine violation.

## Consequence

PDF research records can live under `opt/` (Isolate, PoT 1.1) without a PoT change and without any lint reading their content. A stray file under `opt/_research/` fails verify with one named error (confirmed: 1 error on a probe file, 0 errors without it). Every other `opt/` path keeps the markdown-only rule.
